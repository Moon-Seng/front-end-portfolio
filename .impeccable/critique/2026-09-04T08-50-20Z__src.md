## Overall Impression

The Field Manual direction is memorable and coherent, but the experience currently sells the metaphor faster than it sells Moon Seng. The biggest opportunity is to make name, seniority, ownership, and proof unmistakable within the first scan while preserving the authored system.

## What's Working

- The field-manual language is carried through structure, typography, color, controls, and interaction rather than applied as decoration.
- The career route creates a more distinctive inspection model than a standard timeline.
- The contact section has direct email, availability, validation, loading, success, and failure feedback.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Identity and seniority are too implicit in the first viewport.** The hero foregrounds “MS / FIELD MANUAL” and “Building interfaces that hold up,” while “Moon Seng” is not visibly stated and seniority is embedded in a stylized record (`src/App.tsx:70,81-92`). Add the full name and explicit role/seniority to the primary text hierarchy.
- **[P1] The first viewport does not fulfill the route-led brief.** The direction contract calls for the 2022-present career route to anchor the first viewport, but the route begins after the hero (`src/.impeccable/surfaces/src-app-tsx.md`, `src/App.tsx:81-101`). Bring a compact route index into the hero or revise the contract.
- **[P1] Project records are not yet decision-grade evidence.** Cards and overlays expose descriptions, features, tags, and links, but not Moon Seng’s verified role, constraints, process, or outcomes (`src/App.tsx:115,131`). Add verified case-study fields for selected projects, or label the records as capability summaries until those facts exist.
- **[P2] Important content is hidden behind metaphorical IA.** About and testimonials are not first-class navigation destinations, while “Field notes” and “Open channel” require interpretation (`src/App.tsx:71-75,118-126`). Add direct labels or visible routes.
- **[P2] Custom interaction semantics are incomplete.** Career items declare tabs without a matching `tabpanel`, `aria-controls`, or arrow-key model; project records open a dialog without visible Escape handling, focus trapping, or focus restoration (`src/App.tsx:100-104,131`).

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, hiring manager:** The first viewport does not state “Moon Seng” plainly; career proof is deferred; “senior” lacks ownership context; testimonials are easy to miss.
- **Sam, potential client:** “Selected systems” does not distinguish Moon Seng’s contribution from broader project scope; “message intake” may make inquiry feel heavier than necessary.
- **Casey, mobile/fast scanner:** Large display type and long records increase scan cost; anchor targets may land beneath the sticky header; overlays may not be keyboard-dismissable or focus-safe.
